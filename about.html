🎯 Hamara Goal

Ek aisi community banana jaha sab milke decide karein ki aage kya banana hai. Idea kisi ka bhi ho, vote sabka hoga.

📜 Rules

  1. Sabki izzat karo, koi gaali-galoch ya bullying nahi.
  2. Spam aur fake links mana hain.
  3. Idea do, sirf criticism mat karo.
  4. Decision vote se hoga, kisi ek ka nahi.
  5. Seekhne wale ko help karo, sab kabhi beginner the.

🚀 Kaise kaam karta hai

1. Join kar  →  2. Idea daal  →  3. Sab vote karein  →  4. Jeetne wala idea banta hai.
